# Kinsong Pets (prototype)

A quiet memorial garden by the Rainbow Bridge for a pet who has passed. Not a chatbot: a place to visit on the days that matter.

Why pets come first: see [docs/MEMORIAL_GARDEN.md §8](../docs/MEMORIAL_GARDEN.md). It's the lowest-risk, highest-acceptance way into the Western market. There are no publicity or biometric-consent issues, and "they're happy over the bridge" is already part of the culture.

## What it does

| Screen | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Setup** | Name, species (dog, cat, rabbit, bird, other), coat and marking colours, the day they passed, an optional birthday and gotcha day, and their favorite toys, treats, spots and personality. |
| **Garden: they live there** | Your pet lives in the meadow on its own: it wanders, sniffs the flowers you planted, naps under the memory tree, plays with its favorite toy, and watches the rainbow. How often it does each depends on species and the words you used ("lazy" naps more, "goofy" plays more). After 9 pm it sleeps under the stars. |
| **Garden: interact** | **Pet** them (button, or tap them), **call** their name (they come to you), **play** with their toy, **give a treat** (they go to the bowl and eat). Each reaction plays one of **your own recordings** (a bark when called, a purr when petted), with little hearts. |
| **Memories: videos** | Add short **video clips** (up to 8 s each, trimmed in the picker) and tag what the pet is doing in each: sitting, walking, playing, barking, sleeping, eating. The phone cuts the pet out of every frame (Apple Vision, iOS 17+) and saves the result as a see-through video (HEVC with alpha) at the footage's own frame rate. The crop follows the pet like a camera operator, so a dog walking across the room becomes a steady walk on the spot. From then on the garden shows **the real pet from your footage** instead of the storybook figure: it plays the clip that matches what it's doing, crossfading between clips, and a barking clip barks with its own sound, in step with its mouth. It only walks across the meadow when there's a walking clip, at a steady walking pace and facing the way it goes (one tap flips a clip if it faces the wrong way); otherwise it stays where it is. Close-ups, where the pet runs off the bottom of the picture, are shown large and near, rising from the bottom of the scene. The real pet lives in a **real meadow**: a photograph, with the memory tree, mailbox, bowl, ball, candles and planted flowers all cut out of real photos and standing at their real size for how far away they are; the rainbow is a faint real-looking one behind the treeline, and the family's photos are laid out as prints under the scene. At night the meadow, the things in it and the footage are all dimmed to moonlight, with stars, the moon and candlelight. Photo credits: `assets/garden/CREDITS.md`. Captions describe what the footage shows. If the pet can't be separated from the background, the clip shows in a soft frame instead. |
| **Play with them (the garden as a game)** | One photo of the pet standing, seen from the side, is enough. The phone cuts it out and finds its skeleton with Apple Vision's animal body pose (nose, eyes, ears, neck, every leg's joints and paws, tail), then makes the photo itself into a real-time puppet (SpriteKit): a fine warp grid tied to bones, so the real photo breathes, turns its head, wags, walks and gallops. It lives in any of the six photographed scenes, at its real size for its distance: it rests, wanders off to sniff, invites you to play, and sleeps at night. **Stroke it** and it leans into your finger with hearts; **tap the ground** and it comes over; **flick the ball** and it chases it, picks it up and brings it back to your feet; the buttons call it, pet it, throw the ball and give a treat. Its real bark and recordings play along. An optional photo of it asleep is used at night (a standing photo can't be bent into lying down convincingly). Nothing leaves the phone. Labelled "moving as a puppet… Imagined, not filmed". |
| **Memories: bring them to life** | Opt-in. After the family agrees (and is told plainly what leaves the phone), they choose a photo that shows the pet head to paws, or one of their videos. The phone cuts the pet out and places it in one of six photographed scenes (summer meadow, beach at sunset, autumn woods, lupine meadow, mountain lake, first snow), matching it to the scene's light with a soft contact shadow. That key frame goes to our server (`server/`, which holds the video service's key) and a video model (Runware) makes nine 5-second clips that start and end on it: resting, barking, wandering off and back, playing, being petted, nosing the ground, lying down, sleeping and getting up. They're downloaded and kept on the phone. In the garden the pet then lives in that scene full-frame: it rests on a loop, wanders and plays now and then, sleeps after dark, and reacts when called or petted, every clip joining the next on the same frame so there are no visible cuts; their real bark or recordings play with it. Flowers and candles stand in the scene's perspective but never over the pet. Always labelled "Brought to life by AI… Imagined, not filmed". |
| **Memories: their place** | Instead of the meadow, the real pet can live **somewhere it loved**: choose a photo of your garden, the park, its spot by the window (cropped to the garden's shape on the phone), then drag a line to where the ground meets the distance. The pet's own cut-out stands in the preview near and far at the size it will be, so it's easy to get right; everything in the garden is sized to that photo's perspective. At night the photo is dimmed to moonlight. "Back to the meadow" undoes it. |
| **Home screen widget** | The pet's first frame from its own video, standing in its place, with the next day to remember ("Max's birthday · in 12 days", "Tomorrow: …", or the day itself). It counts down on its own, without the app. Prefers a calm, whole-body clip. iOS 17+, needs the installed app. |
| **Memories** | Add **photos**: they hang as polaroids in the garden, open full-screen with a slow drift and one of their sounds, and the first one sets the storybook pet's coat and marking colours automatically ("Use colours" on any photo). Add **recordings** and tag each (bark, whine, purr, meow, chirp, happy…) so the right sound plays for each reaction. Sounds can be switched off. |
| **Garden: leave something** | An illustrated meadow by the Rainbow Bridge. **Light a candle** (lit for the day), **plant a flower** (stays forever and grows a little each year), **leave a treat** (shows for a week), **write a letter** (goes in the mailbox). The memory tree gains a ring for every year you come back. On remembrance days a gentle banner appears, with an imagined note "from" them built only from what you wrote. |
| **Wishes** | Things they never got to do ("chase the seagulls at the beach"). Each wish gets an **imagined postcard** (beach, snow, forest, lake, sky or meadow, chosen from the words), always labelled as imagined. When you do it for them in real life, mark it done. |
| **Days** | **Remind me on these days** (off until you turn it on): one gentle local notification at 10 am on each remembrance day of the coming year, rescheduled every time the app opens. Never on ordinary days, nothing sent off the phone. Upcoming remembrance days: birthday, gotcha day, the anniversary of their passing, National Pet Day (Apr 11), Rainbow Bridge Remembrance Day (Aug 28) and National Pet Memorial Day (2nd Sunday of Sept). Also a journal of every visit, editing, and deletion. |

## Product rules (enforced in `core/src/garden.ts`)

- **Nothing is for sale.** Flowers, candles, treats and letters are always free, with no rarity and no "more gifts = happier pet".
- **No streaks, no guilt.** The only nudge is on a remembrance day (`gentleNudge` returns nothing on ordinary days).
- **Imagined things are labelled.** Postcards and notes are always marked as imagined. The pet is a storybook illustration in an illustrated garden unless you add video clips; then it is your own footage, cut out of its background, never generated or retouched, in a photographed meadow. Only if the family opts in to "bring them to life" is the pet's movement generated, and then every scene says so.
- **The garden only grows.** Nothing withers if you stay away.
- **They're always content.** The pet never gets hungry, lonely or sad and never "waits" for you, so nothing depends on how long you've been away (`core/src/petLife.ts`, tested).
- **Their real voice, untouched.** Recordings, and the sound of video clips, are played back as-is; nothing is synthesised. Captions describe what the simulated pet does ("Max comes running"); the pet doesn't talk, except in the clearly labelled imagined notes.
- **Labelled as a simulation** under the scene.
- **Everything stays on the phone** (AsyncStorage for data; photos and recordings in the app's documents folder on iOS/Android; small data URIs in the web preview). The one exception is opt-in: "bring them to life" sends the key frame of each chosen scene to our server and the video service, after saying so.

## Run it

```sh
cd ../core && npm install
cd ../app && npm install
npm run web          # preview in a browser
npm test             # 13 app tests; garden and pet-life logic tests live in ../core/test/{garden,petLife}.test.ts
npx expo start       # Expo Go works for everything except video clips
npx expo run:ios     # video clips and the widget need the native build (modules/pet-cutout, targets/widget)
```

## Not in the prototype yet

- **New movement the footage doesn't show.** With video clips the pet looks exactly like the real one but only does what the clips show. Generating new actions in its likeness (an animal "live portrait" or video model) needs a model that is too heavy for most phones today; the plan is an opt-in step on a family-run computer, or on-device once models shrink. Same consent and deletion rules as everything else.
- Video cut-outs on Android (the native module is iOS only; Android shows the storybook pet).
- Sharing a garden with family (end-to-end-encrypted sync), and exporting a memory book.
- Real-world tie-ins from the research: flowers delivered to a real resting place, planting a real tree.
- Localisation (English only), a date picker instead of typed dates, and a designed icon (`assets/icon.png` is a placeholder).
